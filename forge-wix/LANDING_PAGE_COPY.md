## FAQ

### Do I need a commercial gym?
No. Your assessment records the equipment you actually have, and the training system selects from the available exercise library accordingly.

### Is every day a hard workout?
No. FORGE can prescribe recovery / mobility work on non-lifting days and can reduce training stress when recent recovery data calls for it.

### What if I miss a workout?
You continue forward. FORGE does not stack multiple hard sessions simply to punish a missed day.

### What if something hurts?
Use the pain flag and describe the issue. The training system is designed to prioritize conservative modification over progression when pain is reported. It does not diagnose injuries or replace appropriate medical evaluation.

### Do I have to lose a certain amount of weight to complete FORGE?
No. Completion is based on adherence. Weight and measurements are optional progress metrics.

### Can I cancel FORGE Continuation?
Continuation is a separate recurring membership and follows the cancellation terms shown during its checkout.
