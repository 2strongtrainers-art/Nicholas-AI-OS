Hermes HTTP endpoint headless deployment contract

- Existing SWITCHBOARD_API_KEY remains unchanged during headless deploys.
- Public /hermes/jobs routes require the existing GPT bearer.
- Internal /hermes/internal/* routes require a separate Hermes worker bearer.
- The Hermes worker bearer is stored only in ~/.config/nicholas-ai-switchboard/hermes-worker.key with owner-only permissions.
- Internal routes are not present in the OpenAPI schema.
- The Mac worker remains read-only and does not expose arbitrary shell execution.
