# Producer and review

## Interview and content package

Use the guest or topic, intended audience, format, and publication goal. Make a reasonable assumption for a missing low-stakes input and label it. Verify public roles and factual claims from primary material when available. Build an angle and short interview arc, a private prep guide with follow-ups, and a guest-safe outline ready to review. Track open claims separately. Do not contact the guest during prep.

Use an authorized recording or transcript for repurposing. Produce a cut list with exact time references, a spoken script or intro, three title and thumbnail concepts, and the newsletter or social drafts requested. Preserve the speaker's meaning and mark uncertain transcript lines. Source or credit third-party work and keep the owner's review of titles, visual claims, and external copy.

## Trend scout

Use the method in [Weekly review](weekly.md). If X or YouTube sampling fails, show the evidence you did observe and avoid a gap claim. Supply a testable angle and source credit. Broad search index counts do not measure demand.

## Timestamped video review

Use the local reviewer at `chief_of_staff/review.html` in the Nicholas AI OS repository, or the copy explicitly supplied by the owner. The reviewer opens a local video and exports timestamped notes as JSON or text. If given notes, confirm the asset and edit version, quote the relevant time markers, and turn notes into a proposed edit list. Timestamps refer to the selected cut. A timestamp comment alone does not define a safe source trim interval.

Use the existing Workflow Router and OpenMontage or CapCut route when the owner asks to edit and provides the correct media. For OpenMontage Fast Reel, translate only unambiguous trim ranges to its existing edit decision format. Before claiming a render, verify the artifact and the existing worker's QA result. For long-form or unresolved notes, return the editorial handoff with owner review.

## Optional personal check-ins

For a training check-in, read the owner's supplied training log, compare consistent periods, and distinguish missing sessions from missed sessions. For a local weekend shortlist, use the owner's supplied area and preferences, verify dates, hours, availability, and price from event organizers, and link each result. Run these on request or on a dedicated owner-selected schedule.
