# Chief of Staff — Premium social carousel and visual storytelling standard (v1.0)

**Owner approved 2026-11-14.** Scope: all TriValley.fit, TriValley Consultants, ARGUS PRIME, market, research, and fitness social graphics and carousels. This is the **canonical reusable creative brief**, not a one-off Nov 14 news script. Use when generating, auditing, revising, or publishing these assets; preserve the separate brand and topic identity of each.

## Durable decisions from the ARGUS PRIME national security × markets carousel

- Target **professional broadcast/editorial quality** with approachable authority, not a wall of repeated graphics: luxury dark navy/black, warm gold/bronze, crisp ivory typography, restrained red/teal status colors, subtle Trinity-knot brandmark and fine lines. Apply brand-specific naming: TriValley.fit / Nick Style 3 for fitness; TriValley Consultants for business; ARGUS PRIME for OSINT. Do NOT stamp ARGUS PRIME on unrelated fitness content.
- **Strong specific opening**: one defensible headline/hook, then a why-care sentence and a visible swipe cue. The effective ARGUS example is "SIX MINUTES BEFORE THE BELL"; avoid generic "NEWS UPDATE" headlines.
- Default 4:5 **1080 × 1350** for Instagram feed; choose **6–8 slides** based on message density (eight for the approved Nov 14 case). No more than **one central takeaway per slide**. Convert dense two-column comparisons into individual slides; leave nuance and citations in caption and evidence ledger, not microscopic print.
- Build a genuine **story arc**: Hook → primary timeline → investigative proof → data visualization → competing explanation/debunk → distinct contextual risk chapters → evidence ladder → specific watchlist and save/ask CTA. Vary layout by purpose: portraits, timelines, original document quotes, honest charts, evidence tables, maps only when materially relevant.
- Preserve the **owner's facial likeness** from authorized uploaded/referenced imagery when requested. Use **varied expressions and angles** (confident, speaking, thoughtful, warm smile) across *select* slides, not the same pose on every frame. Prefer visual storytelling and data-only slides where appropriate. Never claim biometric matching, exact likeness proof, or verified identity from an AI rendering. Do not place personal source photographs or reference images in public GitHub.
- **Mobile readability is a release gate**: large high-contrast fonts, balanced negative space, bounded panels and no portrait covering important words or footer. Test final 1080×1350 exports downscaled to roughly 360×450 display dimensions; if material text becomes illegible, shorten/edit/split until it is readable.
- **Use evidence over decoration**: relevant official excerpts (brief, correctly attributed), sourced charts with correct scale/units/timing and dates, truthful neutral illustration. News illustrations and synthetic portraits are labeled *illustrative/AI-assisted* and never presented as original incident photography or forensic evidence. Do not embed unchecked fake logo variants or invented statistics.
- **Explicit confidence**: documented fact vs reported allegation vs market association vs inference vs unknown. Separate regular-close from premarket/after-hours snapshots, time zones and quote timestamps. Never draw an arrow claiming causation merely because events overlap. State no confirmed joint attack when evidence is absent, not "all incidents ruled out."
- **Accuracy gate before generation**: check current primary sources and dated independent reporting, or explicitly work from the user's supplied source. Create a claim ledger first; reconcile contradictions and stale news. Do not fabricate quotes, sources, prices, official endorsements, certifications, earnings or timelines.
- **After production**: inspect every slide full-size and at phone size; check typography, image crops, likeness consistency, clipping, chronology, citations, screenshot legibility, external logos, and source limitations. Package numbered PNGs, preview image, paste-ready caption (<=2,200 characters for Instagram), separate source ledger, and handoff notes. No auto-posting or paid rendering without authorization and cost guard.
- Aim for **at least 9/10 on design and readability**, with factual accuracy a mandatory independent pass (not averaged into aesthetics). A photogenic image cannot compensate for inaccurate source text.

## Reuse: eight-slide model from the Nov 14 2026 ARGUS iteration

1. **Specific hook** — "6 MINUTES BEFORE THE BELL" and timing context.
2. **Chronology** — 3:36 → 3:54 → 4:00 ET, later corrections; different regions and UTC conversions checked.
3. **Official primary record** — DOT L3Harris identified; 1,000+ connection attempts per 4–5 seconds; exact fault unknown.
4. **Market visualization** — index performance, proportional bar chart, separate after-hours row.
5. **Contradictory evidence** — Microsoft technical change and NWS normal operations undermine common-cause claim.
6. **Hormuz separately** — disputed tanker claims, real shipping risk, Brent/WTI closing comparison.
7. **Nvidia separately** — documented leadership change, export scrutiny, multiple market drivers.
8. **Evidence ladder + next watch** — documented / associated / not verified, dated follow-ups and CTA.

Do not re-use fixed historical facts in a later new carousel without freshness verification. The model is a **visual and workflow template**, not an always-current news feed.

## Operating handoff

1. Chief of Staff/Producer receives content topic and intended channel.
2. Researcher generates source & claim ledger, grouping by confidence and freshness.
3. Creative lead selects a narrative hook, then writes a slide-by-slide storyboard and portrait placement plan.
4. Design/render step produces accurate selectable/programmatic text where feasible, with image generation for the authorized creative imagery.
5. QA reviewer inspects the full sequence and mobile thumbnails, validates claim-to-source mapping, separates allegation from fact, and requires changes until blocking errors cleared.
6. Owner receives numbered PNGs, overview/ZIP, caption, source ledger, and a truthful completion/status report.
