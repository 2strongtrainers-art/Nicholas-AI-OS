# Revenue pilot

Run one bounded experiment. Use an existing verified TriValley offer, one audience with recent evidence of interest, and one measurable action. The first pilot uses the live Elite Assessment booking path and prior assessment or coaching inquiries. Record the source, current price, delivery capacity, exact date window and the permitted budget. Default to existing included tools. Choose a small initial cohort, with up to 15 contacts, and preserve the owner's decision about whether and how to send.

1. Use HubSpot as the source of contact and deal status. If relevant data is unavailable, review the owner's approved Wix inquiries or direct Gmail threads. Use stable source IDs, verify last contact and applicable communication permissions, and exclude opted-out, closed, active-client, duplicate, unrelated or unsupported contacts. A generic marketplace listing or an unsent outbound draft does not show prior interest. Leave private records in their services.
2. Group the evidence by the actual reason the conversation stopped. Distinguish asked for details, timing, price and unknown. Draft a short reply that reflects what the person actually said and offers one next step. Resolve the correct recipient and existing thread. Use the public service and price currently on file. Show the owner only the appropriate number of drafts and a source link for review. Use current applicable outreach requirements and the repo's outbound controls for any later authorized send.
3. Record distinct eligible, contacted, positive replies, booked calls, paid customers and settled orders for the cohort. Verify attribution from approved campaign tags or matched booking and payment evidence. Use payments actually received, refunds, processing, fulfillment, incremental software and other direct cash costs. Record any verified avoided invoice separately from owner time saved, estimated pipeline value or a projected month. An interested reply can later become a booked call. Avoid summing their estimated values as though they were independent revenue.
4. Report a decision at day 10. One paid attributable assessment with positive cash contribution earns a second small batch for owner review. Replies with no paid booking call for checking the offer, timing or booking path. Weak engagement calls for a better qualified list. A missing input is a coverage gap. The local aggregate scorecard can validate complete cash inputs using the existing commerce economics rules.
5. For an internal clipping test, use owned footage, record editor time and any real invoice displaced, and compare performance on the same platform and time window. For a purchasing comparison, compare like-for-like delivered prices, terms, returns and installation. Log a saving only after the lower cost is confirmed. Treat speculative trading, unsupported marketplace automation and unapproved external sends as outside this pilot.
