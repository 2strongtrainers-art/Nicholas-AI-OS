## Start here: a $297 paid content pilot

Offer two original branded motion clips, 8–15 seconds each, one revision per clip and caption drafts. Test four clips/month at $597 only after timing delivery and finding paid demand. The prices are hypotheses, not validated market rates. A sample demonstrates production, not sales or marketing results.

This ranks first because it fits Nick’s coaching/content experience and produces a finished output a buyer can assess without installing a customer-facing AI platform. The coaching workflow ranks second for improving the existing business. Do not create a broad agency or trading service before a paid pilot.

## Detailed paths and seven-day tests

### 1. Content studio

**Customer offer:** Four branded motion clips/month for trainers and local businesses.

**Tools and deliverable requirements:** Hyperframes; Open-Generative-AI optional. One reusable motion template. Preserve the existing OpenMontage/Fast Reel path for supplied-footage edits. Generative imagery, voice and stock are excluded.

**Setup/cost:** budget 4 internal setup hours and 1 initial acquisition hours/client as assumptions. Delivery assumption: 2 hours/unit. Real infrastructure costs require a deployment quote.

**First-customer route and seven-day continue/stop gate:** Show this sample to ten warm business contacts, seek five conversations and one $297 paid two-clip pilot. Continue only if the pilot is usable and takes <=2 hours including one revision.

**Evidence and what may fail:** Strong skills fit, demonstrated sample; no paid customer validation. Customer acquisition, scope and real delivery/support effort must be measured. Stop or redesign if the gate fails.

### 2. Premium coaching workflow

**Customer offer:** The existing $497 online-coaching offer with structured check-ins and visual progress reports.

**Tools and deliverable requirements:** LibreChat optional; Hyperframes optional. Preserve existing Notion/Airtable records. No live client data is imported. LibreChat later needs a host, MongoDB/services and approved model configuration. Hours must include communication and review; no automatic program changes.

**Setup/cost:** budget 6 internal setup hours and 1 initial acquisition hours/client as assumptions. Delivery assumption: 2 hours/unit. Real infrastructure costs require a deployment quote.

**First-customer route and seven-day continue/stop gate:** Have five opt-in prospect conversations. Seek one suitable enrollment at the existing price and time a fictional check-in/report dry run to <=30 minutes. Measure total support time in month one.

**Evidence and what may fail:** Very strong business fit; incremental conversion/retention unmeasured. Customer acquisition, scope and real delivery/support effort must be measured. Stop or redesign if the gate fails.

### 3. Inbox setup service

**Customer offer:** One-domain email drafting workflow, access setup and handoff training for a compatible business.

**Tools and deliverable requirements:** Agentic Inbox. Requires domain-owner access, Cloudflare Access, Email Routing/Service, R2, Durable Objects and Workers AI. Shared Access members can access all mailboxes by design. No deployment, Gmail migration or live sending verified. Provider/domain costs are separately quoted.

**Setup/cost:** budget 10 internal setup hours and 2 initial acquisition hours/client as assumptions. Delivery assumption: 6 hours/unit. Real infrastructure costs require a deployment quote.

**First-customer route and seven-day continue/stop gate:** Interview three compatible domain owners; continue after one paid setup request and a non-production inbound/authentication/draft-review test.

**Evidence and what may fail:** Build/typecheck passed; willingness to pay and live deployment unverified. Customer acquisition, scope and real delivery/support effort must be measured. Stop or redesign if the gate fails.

### 4. Private AI workspace

**Customer offer:** One team workspace answering questions about approved business documents, plus training.

**Tools and deliverable requirements:** LibreChat; VTuber optional; Camofox optional. Requires maintained host, database/services and approved provider/data policy. Camofox browser integration remains unresolved. An avatar adds ASR/TTS/model setup without proving customer value. Hardware and inference billed separately.

**Setup/cost:** budget 12 internal setup hours and 2 initial acquisition hours/client as assumptions. Delivery assumption: 8 hours/unit. Real infrastructure costs require a deployment quote.

**First-customer route and seven-day continue/stop gate:** Interview three owners about one costly repeated task; continue after one paid request and ten representative synthetic-document queries pass with citations/access checks.

**Evidence and what may fail:** Setup-service hypothesis; support load and hardware needs unresolved. Customer acquisition, scope and real delivery/support effort must be measured. Stop or redesign if the gate fails.

### 5. Paper-research journal

**Customer offer:** A research journal, fee/slippage worksheet and paper-trade review template for record-keeping.

**Tools and deliverable requirements:** Vibe-Trading; AutoHedge optional; Fincept excluded commercially pending license clearance. Use synthetic/public records only. Fincept has custom commercial/internal-use license terms. No profitable strategy is validated. Sell bookkeeping utility, not signals, personalized recommendations or returns. No live trading.

**Setup/cost:** budget 8 internal setup hours and 1 initial acquisition hours/client as assumptions. Delivery assumption: 2 hours/unit. Real infrastructure costs require a deployment quote.

**First-customer route and seven-day continue/stop gate:** Show a synthetic example to three journal users. Continue only after one paid request, reproducible fee calculations and applicable license/marketing review.

**Evidence and what may fail:** Weakest revenue evidence; no demand or trading edge validated. Customer acquisition, scope and real delivery/support effort must be measured. Stop or redesign if the gate fails.

## Avoid low-margin support

Inbox support at $99/month, $15 direct cost and one support hour leaves $34 at $50/hour but −$116 at $200/hour. AI-workspace support at $149/month, $20 cost and 1.5 hours leaves $54 at $50/hour but −$171 at $200/hour. A $49 research subscription with $5 costs and one work hour is already negative at $50/hour; reject it unless scope and effort change. These are sensitivity examples, not offers to publish.
