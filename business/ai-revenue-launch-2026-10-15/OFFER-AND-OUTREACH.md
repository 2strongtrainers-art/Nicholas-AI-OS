2. Collect approved message, CTA, branding and format; no client health records.
3. Duplicate the template and adapt copy/colors/timing.
4. Run Hyperframes check, then render with the pinned CLI.
5. Inspect decoded frames; verify dimensions, duration, fps and codec.
6. Deliver two MP4s and caption drafts privately through an approved channel.
7. Complete the one revision, record time and ask about ongoing usefulness.
