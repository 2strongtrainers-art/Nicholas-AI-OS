How to use it

Use the drawing controls on the map to add points, lines and polygons. Polygon acreage and line mileage are calculated automatically.

Elevation: tap the map while not drawing to query USGS elevation at that location.

Field-to-GIS workflow

Avenza → this app

Export your Avenza map features as KML/KMZ, GPX, CSV, or Shapefile ZIP. On iPhone, save the export to Files, then tap Import GIS files here and choose it directly.

Google Earth Pro → this app

Save Places as KML or KMZ, then import the file. Names, descriptions, points, lines and polygons are carried into the project where possible.

ArcGIS / QGIS interoperability

Export GeoJSON or KML from this app for use in ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Online, QGIS and Google Earth. The original imported attributes remain attached to mapped features when supported.

Privacy

The MVP processes project files in your browser and autosaves locally on the device. It does not require an account or upload your project to a database.
