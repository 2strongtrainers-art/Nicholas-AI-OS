# Coinbase Crypto Futures Research — 2026-08-25

Current Coinbase documentation shows Advanced Trade supports US derivatives futures through FUTURE products, including futures-specific balance/position and margin endpoints. Public Advanced Trade market-data endpoints support product discovery, candles, ticker snapshots, and product metadata. Product metadata includes futures contract size, contract expiry type, 24/7 status, funding information, and maximum leverage fields.

Coinbase's current US derivatives materials state that crypto futures trade 24/7 with a Friday 5–6 PM ET maintenance break. Current perpetual-style contract specifications include nano BTC (BIP, 0.01 BTC contract size) and nano ETH (ETP, 0.10 ETH contract size), among other crypto products.

Paper v1 deliberately restricts itself to BTC/ETH and public market data. No account authentication or order submission is implemented.
