# TriValley.fit Youth Athletic Development Academy — prelaunch handoff

**Status:** `BUILT / REVIEW REQUIRED` (public-safe source and operational checklist only). **No live enrollment, payments, school credit, medical treatment, permission to work with minors, or website publishing is authorized by these files.** Snapshot: 2026-11-17 America/Los_Angeles.

## Why this exists

Develop the child first, the athlete second, and sport-specific abilities when appropriate. First cohort concept: ages 6–12; expandable K–12 scope. Multi-sport physical literacy, movement confidence, positive social behavior, safe resistance-training foundations where appropriate, and enjoyment. Never advertise weight-loss transformations, maximal lifting, concussion care, scholarship outcomes or guaranteed injury prevention.

Three **proposed** readiness-adjusted bands:

| Track | Age guide | Concept session duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explorers | 6–8 | 45 minutes |
| Builders | 9–10 | 55 minutes |
| Connectors | 11–12 | 60 minutes |

Proposed cadence is 12 weeks × two sessions/week, 24 core lessons. `academy-curriculum.public.json` exposes the high-level session topics; it is not the private coaching manual, medical record, child enrollment database or production app.

## Publishing and safeguarding — fail-closed acceptance

Before opening registration or publishing promises:

1. Qualified California legal review of youth-service organization duties, mandatory reporting and screening/training (including BPC §18975 / AB506 applicability), guardian consent, privacy (COPPA/CCPA where applicable), refund and program classifications.
2. Appropriate business/abuse/molestation and athletic insurance validated by the policy broker, written facility permissions and accessibility plan.
3. Two screened, appropriately trained adults in a visible supervised setting, with no unobserved one-on-one contact; capacity determined by child needs, experience and venue, not assumed legal ratios.
4. California HSC §124235 concussion/SCA procedures, annual required parent forms/coach education and remove-from-play rules, documented emergency escalation, incident records in a restricted system.
5. California HSC §124238.5 scope checked against the **Jan 1, 2027** CPR/AED-certification and cardiac emergency plan milestone, and **Jan 1, 2028** AED accessibility milestone. AB 387 requires facility/youth organization access coordination and cost-sharing discussion when the facility has installed an AED; if the facility has none, the youth organization must ensure an operational AED is available during the entire practice/match. Confirm current consolidated text and legal scope before launch.
6. Staff-site emergency rehearsal for medical events, heat/smoke/AQI, reunification, missing child, behavioral safety and accessible activities.
7. Review by qualified youth S&C/physical education and pediatric/adaptive consultants for developmentally appropriate activity adaptations when needed.
8. Adult-only interest experience, privacy-reviewed forms, verified dates/costs, clear parent terms, accessible mobile site and documented owner approval.
9. No automatic use of children's images, voice or health details. Separate guardian photo/video consent and opt-out; no child tracking, bios or performance rankings on public pages.
10. Two staffed synthetic/adult-only lesson rehearsals and concrete QA acceptance evidence before first real cohort.
