## Proposed internal goals — not 99th-percentile benchmarks
- 100% required safety gate approval prior to participation, with zero critical behavioral breaches.
- Coach rubric: 5 critical observable safeguarding standards plus 11 noncritical instruction/inclusion standards; target at least 90% of actually observed noncritical items across two adult-only rehearsals.
- Two-coach movement-anchor calibration goal: at least 80% exact agreement; zero means Not Observed rather than a skill deficit.
- After all approvals, one pilot 12-week cohort: record denominators and missingness; exploratory targets attendance >=85%, positive voluntary enjoyment >=80% of responders, parent trust >=4.5/5, paired skill observation completeness >=90%, eligible paid renewal >=60%; no false causal or national-percentile claims.
- Parent discovery: 15–20 opt-in adults; collect candid price/schedule feedback, preserving unfavorable answers and acknowledging convenience sampling.
