    Read the TriValley pilot data notice (TVFIT-PILOT-2026-11-V1)

    TriValley.fit's private coaching pilot stores each opted-in member's Wix member ID and display name, assigned programs, workout dates and durations, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, and optional check-in comments. It does not request clinical histories, medical records, or diagnoses.

    A client sees their own records; the explicitly assigned coach sees that client's coached progress and check-ins. The owner can export encrypted backups and review deletion requests. Coaching information is held in a restricted Supabase database in the U.S. West region. Backup exports are encrypted locally using a password held by the owner.

    Records remain until reviewed for deletion. Deletion from the live coaching database also removes its in-database revision history, but previously downloaded backup files must be separately managed. The Supabase Free plan does not provide guaranteed automatic backups and can pause for inactivity. This is a limited coaching pilot, not an emergency or medical service.

    The owner is responsible for informing participating clients, protecting exported files and their passwords, reviewing requests, and checking privacy and legal requirements before wider use.
